Pāhoehoe lava is thinner than a'a, and flows more readily. Its surface looks more wrinkly and smooth. Pāhoehoe lava flows in a series of lobes that form strange twisted shapes and natural rock sculptures ( Figure below ). Pāhoehoe lava can form lava tubes. The outer layer of the lava flow cools and solidifies. The inner part of the flow remains fluid. The fluid lava flows through and leaves behind a tube ( Figure below ).
