Glucose is a simple carbohydrate with the chemical formula C 6 H 12 O 6 . It stores chemical energy in a concentrated, stable form. In your body, glucose is the form of energy that is carried in your blood and taken up by each of your trillions of cells. Glucose is the end product of photosynthesis, and it is the nearly universal food for life.
