It could easily be said that a cell is the fundamental unit of life, the smallest unit capable of life or the structural and functional unit necessary for life. But whatever it is, a cell is necessary for life. This concept will discuss some of the fundamental properties of the cell with lessons that include the cell structure, transport in and out of the cell and cell communication.
