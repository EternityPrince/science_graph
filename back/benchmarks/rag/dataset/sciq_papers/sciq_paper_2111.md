The standard reduction potential can be determined by subtracting the standard reduction potential for the reaction occurring at the anode from the standard reduction potential for the reaction occurring at the cathode. The minus sign is necessary because oxidation is the reverse of reduction. E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode +0.34 V = E° 2+ − E°H + /H = E° 2+ − 0 = E° Cu.
