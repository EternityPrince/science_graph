8.2 Chromatin Chromosomes consist of chromatin11 . This is made up of strings of DNA, which typically measure centimeters in length if stretched out. This DNA is wound around a histone12 core and organized into nucleosome13 s. The chromatin14 must be uncoiled for gene expression15 and replication16 . Chromosome micrograph.
