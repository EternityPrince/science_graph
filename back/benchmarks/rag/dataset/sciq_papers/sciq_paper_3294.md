By giving birth to tiny embryos, marsupial mothers are at less risk. However, the tiny newborn marsupial may be less likely to survive than a newborn placental mammal. The marsupial embryo completes its growth and development outside the mother’s body in a pouch. It gets milk by sucking on a nipple in the pouch. There are very few living species of marsupials.
