Most plant cells have a large central vacuole . It can make up as much as 90 percent of a plant cell’s total volume. The central vacuole is like a large storage container. It may store substances such as water, enzymes, and salts. It may have other roles as well. For example, the central vacuole helps stems and leaves hold their shape. It may also contain pigments that give flowers their colors.
