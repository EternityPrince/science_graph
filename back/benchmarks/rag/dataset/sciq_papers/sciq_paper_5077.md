Pluto was different in other ways. The outer planets are all gas giants. Pluto is small, icy, and rocky. Pluto has a diameter of about 2,400 km. It has only about one-fifth the mass of Earth’s Moon. Pluto’s orbit is tilted relative to the other planets. It's orbit is shaped like a long, narrow ellipse. Pluto’s orbit sometimes even passes inside Neptune’s orbit.
